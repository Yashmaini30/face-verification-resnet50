    identities                  : 2,996
    of those seen in training   : 1,206 (40.3%)

    leakage-free balanced     : AUC 0.8325  EER 25.00%  (6,000 pairs)

  MLFW is adversarial by construction: the same identity wears different
  masks while different identities wear the same mask, on top of CALFW's
  cross-age gap. This model saw no masked faces in training, so near-chance
  on the official protocol is the expected outcome rather than a defect.

==============================================================================
11. HOW TO READ THESE NUMBERS
==============================================================================
  Verification is 1:1 - compare two photos, clear a threshold. Identification
  is 1:N - beat every other enrolled person. Identification is harder, which is
  why Rank-1 is well below verification accuracy on the same embedding.

  Raising the threshold lowers false accepts and raises false rejects; lowering
  it does the reverse. Both cannot fall together at a fixed embedding - only a
  better embedding lifts the whole ROC curve.

  TAR at FAR = 1e-05 rests on about five impostor pairs even in the exhaustive
  protocol, so treat it as a plot endpoint rather than a reliable measurement.

  Trained on ~7,000 images, orders of magnitude fewer than production systems.
  LFW skews towards light-skinned adult males in frontal, well-lit poses, so
  these figures are NOT evidence of fairness across demographics.
