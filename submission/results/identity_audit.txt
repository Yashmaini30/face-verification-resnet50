IDENTITY LABEL AUDIT
split: test   identities: 120   images: 1031

Two things can be wrong with identity labels, and both are detectable from
the embeddings alone:

  A. ONE LABEL, TWO PEOPLE   - a folder holding more than one individual.
     Detected as low mean similarity between images inside that folder.
  B. TWO LABELS, ONE PERSON  - the same individual under two names.
     Detected as two folder templates sitting unusually close together.

==============================================================================
HOW THE PIPELINE HANDLES THEM
==============================================================================
  Pair generation trusts the folder: same folder -> genuine, different
  folders -> impostor. So:

    case A turns some GENUINE pairs into impostor pairs  -> scores too low
    case B turns some IMPOSTOR pairs into genuine pairs  -> scores too high
             for those pairs, i.e. they look like false accepts

  Both push the measured numbers in the PESSIMISTIC direction: A depresses
  the genuine distribution, B inflates the apparent impostor tail. Neither
  can flatter the model - a label error cannot invent a correct match.

  Exact duplicate images are checked separately and rejected outright:
    exact duplicate images found in this split: 0
    (check_leakage.py runs the same test across the whole dataset)
